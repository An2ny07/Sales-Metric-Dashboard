# Sales Metric Dashboard
A simple and beginner-friendly Sales Metrics Dashboard project designed to explore sales data, track key performance indicators, and practice data visualization skills. Built to improve understanding of sales trends, revenue performance, and customer insights using clean and organized visuals.

## Dashboard Overview:
The Sales Metrics Dashboard highlights:
- Revenue trends by month
- Top-performing products
- Total sales, revenue, and profit metrics
- Unit sold overview
- Regional profit distribution
- Order-type segmentation (In-store vs. Online)
- Year-over-year sales comparison

## Project Objective:
The objective of this project is to develop a fully interactive and visually cohesive Power BI dashboard that consolidates essential sales metrics including monthly revenue trends, total sales and profit performance, top selling products, regional profit distribution, order type insights, and year-over-year comparisons into a single analytical platform designed to enhance business transparency, support strategic decision-making, streamline reporting processes, and empower stakeholders with accurate, real-time data for improved operational and financial planning.

## Questions (KPIs):
- What are our total sales, revenue, and profit?
- How many units did we sell overall?
- Which months had the highest and lowest revenue?
- Which products are our top sellers?
- Which region is performing the best?
- Do we sell more through in-store or online orders?
- How does this year’s sales compare to last year?

## Project Insights
- Strong overall performance is shown with high total sales, revenue, and profit, indicating a healthy business trend.
- Revenue trends reveal monthly fluctuations, with some months performing significantly better, helping identify seasonal patterns or marketing impacts.
- Top-selling products such as Highlighters, Folders, Pencils, and Markers drive most of the sales volume, suggesting which items should be prioritized for stock and promotion.
- Regional analysis shows uneven profit distribution, highlighting which areas are outperforming and which may require strategic improvements.
- Order-type breakdown indicates clear customer preferences, showing whether online or in-store channels generate more units sold.
- Year-over-year comparison helps track growth, showing how current performance aligns with previous years’ trends and business goals.
- Category performance by order type highlights product strengths across channels, helping guide operations and marketing strategies.

## Final Conclusion
The Sales Metrics Dashboard provides a clear, data-driven view of the company’s performance across products, regions, and time periods. By combining KPIs, visual trends, and interactive filters, the dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly understand what drives sales, where opportunities lie, and how performance evolves over time. This tool supports more informed decision-making, improves reporting efficiency, and empowers the organization to plan future strategies with greater confidence and clarity.
